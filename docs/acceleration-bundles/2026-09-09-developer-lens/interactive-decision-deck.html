What the project is

Four compatible identities, with one recommended near-term hierarchy.

Personal retrospective

Private, aesthetically rich Story/Wrapped experience for understanding rhythms, themes and project shape.

Maintainer observatory

The strongest near-term standalone product: system flow, integration tail, coverage, feedback and structural change.

Evidence platform

Claims, limitations, lineage, counter-evidence, projections and corrections as first-class product data.

Research workbench pair

Product owns stable contracts; Lab owns experiments, model cards, benchmarks and honest rejection.

Portfolio flagship

A convincing demonstration of systems thinking, privacy engineering, contract design and analytical restraint.

Not a leaderboard

No universal productivity score, human-value judgement or public individual comparison should become the centre.

Strongest work

Privacy and evidence are executable

Synthetic public canaries, sink boundaries, closed registries, hashes, rollback, deletion lineage and explicit unsupported claims make the trust posture unusually credible.

Main weakness

Portfolio truth is distributed

Roadmaps compensate for missing GitHub milestones, while review residuals, activation preconditions and future research look equally “open” in the issue list.

Architecture risk

Two data planes

The stable Story dashboard still uses whole-file data while V2 owns SQLite and evidence. Use a strangler approach: all new System/Research work goes through V2.

Focus rule

70 / 20 / 10

For H0–H2: 70% product/data path, 20% adjacent hardening, 10% governance. A new permanent mechanism must replace something or close a recurring failure.
